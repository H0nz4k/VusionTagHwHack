# EXP-042 — post-cc-tool CP2102 UART framing

## Hypothesis

High-bit UART garbage after isolated flash is CP2102 framing after debugger USB cycle, not MCU firmware. Known-good `v0.5c` hex and a 115200 recapture after settle decode cleanly.

## Method

No new firmware. Reflash existing `v0.5c_rf_rxrssi.hex`. Isolated capture FAIL. Baud sweep 9600/38400/57600/115200/230400 (6 s each, TAG power-cycle). Then `ov26-uart-recap.sh` 18 s at 115200.

## Result

| Baud | ASCII_RATIO | EXP-036 |
|---|---|---|
| 9600 | 0.200 | 0 |
| 38400 | 0.508 | 0 |
| 57600 | 0.234 | 0 |
| **115200** | **0.996** | **1** (DONE not yet, 6 s short) |
| 230400 | 0.209 | 0 |

18 s recapture at 115200: **PASS** `EXP-036` + `DONE`.

Isolated flash UART right after `cc-tool` remains flaky (high-bit burst). Recapture after settle is the decoder of record.
