# EXP-046 — 1 Hz LED while TWN4 in range

## Hypothesis

EXP-045 FD is a short pulse (two blinks on first UID). Polling P1_1 during the delay, with sticky hold, keeps 1 Hz LED (0.5 s on / 0.5 s off) for as long as SearchTag keeps seeing the tag.

## Firmware

`v0.7b_nfc_fd_1hz`. Debugger isolated at runtime.

## Result

Isolated UART **PASS**: idle `FD=01` `SAW=00 HOLD=00 LED=00`. Firmware on DEV, TAG ON for human `field-watch`.

Human EXP-045: UID seen, two blinks (FD pulse). v0.7b polls FD during delay + sticky 1 Hz.
