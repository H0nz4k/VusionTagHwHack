OpenVusion 2.6 GU140 diagnostic UART wiring
=============================================

During runtime diagnostics, after programming:

CC2510 P1_6 / physical pin 33  ---->  RX of 3.3-V USB-TTL adapter
CC2510 AGND / board GND        ---->  GND of USB-TTL adapter

DO NOT CONNECT:
- USB-TTL VCC / 5 V / 3.3 V power output
- classic ±12 V RS-232

The tag remains powered from its own battery/supply.

UART:
115200 baud
8 data bits
no parity
1 stop bit
no flow control

Why P1_6?
- USART1 Alternative 2 TX maps there.
- It is also a candidate flash-MOSI signal.
- Diagnostic firmware forces FLASH_CS (P1_4) HIGH first, so the external flash
  should remain deselected while UART is active.

If you prefer a logic analyzer:
- connect CH0 to P1_6
- GND to GND
- decode Async Serial / UART, 115200, 8N1, idle high.
